Services

Evidence-based therapy tailored to your individual goals, delivered with warmth and without judgement.

Anxiety & Stress

Find relief from racing thoughts, constant worry, and the weight of overwhelm. Anxiety can feel all-consuming, but with the right support, you can learn to manage it effectively.

Together, we'll explore the root causes of your anxiety and develop practical, evidence-based strategies to help you feel calmer and more in control of your life.

Disordered Eating

Heal your relationship with food and learn to eat more intuitively, or overcome your picky eating. Whether you struggle with emotional eating, binge-eating, compulsive eating, restrictive eating, or ARFID, therapy can help.

I'll support you to identify and overcome triggers, decrease obsessive thoughts about food and weight, and tune into hunger and satiety signals for a more peaceful relationship with food.

Body Image

Reduce negative thoughts, anxiety, and shame about your body. Body image struggles can affect every area of your life, from what you wear to how you show up in relationships.

We'll work together to challenge unhelpful beliefs, build self-compassion, and develop a kinder, more accepting relationship with your body.

Depression

Low mood can make everything feel heavy, making it difficult to get out of bed and connect with others. Depression can leave you feeling isolated, hopeless, and exhausted.

Therapy offers a safe space to explore what's underneath, reconnect with what matters, and gradually rebuild a sense of energy and purpose.

Self Worth

Learn to accept yourself more fully and stand up to your internal bully. Low self-worth often develops early and can shape how you move through the world as an adult.

Together, we'll explore the origins of your self-critic and build a stronger, more compassionate relationship with yourself.

Relationships

Understand patterns and ways of being in your relationships and how to keep yourself emotionally safe, without putting up walls.

We'll explore how past experiences shape your relationship patterns and develop new ways of connecting that feel safe, authentic, and fulfilling.

You're In the Right Place If Any of These Resonate With You

There's no right or wrong time to seek support. If something is weighing on you, you deserve a space to explore it.

  • You want a healthier relationship with food that is sustainable for the future
  • You're worried or feeling uncomfortable about weight gain
  • You're starting a GLP-1 and want to set yourself up for success
  • You're considering commencing or coming off a GLP-1 and want an informed, impartial place to discuss this decision
  • You engage in eating patterns you're concerned with, such as emotional eating, binge-eating, or restrictive eating
  • You are struggling with body image or self-compassion
  • You want to build your confidence and self-worth
  • You want to understand deeper issues underpinning your relationship with food and your body
